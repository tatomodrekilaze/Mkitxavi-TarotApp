# Third-party material

The MIT license covers original code. It does not override rights held by other authors.

- Packages listed in `package.json` and `bun.lock` keep their own licenses.
- `scripts/rebuild-rws-deck.mjs` describes the tarot art as scans of Pamela Colman Smith's Rider–Waite–Smith artwork. The checkout lacks a complete source manifest for the scans; verify the particular assets before redistributing them.
- Brand images, the Maria avatar, preview images, and console media do not have individual provenance records in this checkout. Replace assets whose rights you cannot verify before publishing a derivative app.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts. Consult individual font licenses if bundling them yourself.
